About

Professional web design, without the agency bloat.

At Haunted Holdings, we believe every business — no matter the size — deserves a website that works as hard as you do. We're a Raleigh–Durham based web design and digital marketing studio focused on building custom, high-performance sites that drive real results.

We craft digital experiences that are beautiful, fast, and built to be found. Whether you're a local service provider, an e-commerce brand, or a startup finding its footing, we tailor every site to your goals — no templates, no unnecessary upsells.

Our approach is simple: understand your business, design something that works, optimize it for growth, and stick around to support it. From custom design and local SEO to ongoing maintenance, we treat your success like our own.
